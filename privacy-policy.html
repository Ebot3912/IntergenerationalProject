Privacy Policy

Intergenerational Bridge — operated by Small Hands Big Hearts

Effective date: July 7, 2026

This app is available for Android and iOS.

Information We Collect

We collect the following personally identifiable information: email address, user ID, name, age, and location (for emergency SOS features only).

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the app experience
  • To send emergency location alerts when you activate SOS
  • To save your profile, schedule, and contact preferences
  • To power the Compass AI assistant (via Claude API)

AI Features

This app uses an AI assistant (Compass AI) powered by Anthropic's Claude API. Messages you send to Compass AI are processed by Anthropic's servers. We do not store your AI conversations permanently.

Location

Location is only accessed when you activate the SOS emergency feature and is used solely to share your position with your emergency contacts.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase — authentication and data storage
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage analytics
  • Anthropic Claude API — AI assistant
